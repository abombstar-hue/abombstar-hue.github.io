Privacy Policy: A Game of Rope and Mountains

Last updated: November 2026

A Game of Rope and Mountains ("the game") is a single-player game published by ArtsStudios. This policy explains what information is used when you play.

Game progress

Your progress (stars, coins, cards, upgrades and settings) is saved only on your device, inside the app's private storage. We do not run any servers and never receive it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it.

Optional reward videos (Google AdMob, Android version)

The Android version offers optional videos you can choose to watch for an in-game bonus. These videos are provided by Google AdMob. To show them, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's settings at any time.

Supporter pack purchase

The optional Supporter pack is bought through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google under Google's own privacy policy; we never see your payment details. The game only checks with Google Play whether you own the Supporter pack.

PC (Steam) version

The PC version shows no ads and collects no personal information. Purchases are handled by Steam under Valve's privacy policy.

Permissions

The game uses internet access (for videos and purchases) and Google Play Billing. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
